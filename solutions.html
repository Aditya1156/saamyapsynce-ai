Solutions

Six capabilities.
One engineering foundation.

We do not sell a product catalogue. Each engagement is built from the capabilities below, combined in whatever proportion the problem actually demands — and nothing is delivered that cannot be measured.

01 — Applied AI & Machine Learning

Models that earn their place in the workflow.

The hard part of machine learning is rarely the model. It is the data that was never collected, the metric nobody agreed on, and the handover to a team that has to trust the output. We work on all three.

Forecasting & demand modelling

Time-series and causal models for demand, load, capacity and risk, with the uncertainty stated honestly rather than hidden behind a single number.

Anomaly & fraud detection

Detecting the rare event in a stream where almost everything is normal — tuned to the cost of a false positive in your operation, not a generic benchmark.

Document intelligence

Extraction and classification across invoices, claims, contracts and forms, including handwritten and multilingual Indian-language documents.

Domain-tuned language models

Fine-tuning and retrieval-augmented generation over your own corpus, so the system answers from your policies and your data rather than from the open internet.

MLOps & evaluation

Versioning, automated evaluation suites, drift monitoring and retraining pipelines. A model without an evaluation harness is a prototype, not a product.

02 — Computer Vision & Perception

Systems that read the physical world.

Perception is the bridge between an intelligent system and a real environment. It is also what makes immersive interfaces feel natural — interpreting a hand, a glance or a gesture instead of waiting for a button press.

Gesture recognition & hand tracking

Real-time interpretation of hand pose, finger articulation and micro-movement — fluid interaction without a controller in the way.

Inspection & defect detection

Visual quality control on production lines, trained on your defect classes and calibrated to the tolerance your customers enforce.

Scene & spatial understanding

Depth, segmentation and object relationships, so an overlay knows what it is attached to.

Safety & compliance monitoring

PPE verification, restricted-zone alerting and incident capture, built with privacy boundaries set up front.

Edge deployment

Quantised models running on-device where bandwidth, latency or data residency rules out the cloud.

03 — AR & Spatial Computing

Information placed where the work happens.

A manual on a screen interrupts the task. The same instruction anchored to the machine in front of someone becomes part of it.

Guided procedures

Step-by-step assembly, maintenance and changeover instructions anchored to the physical asset, with completion captured automatically.

Remote expert assistance

A specialist sees what the technician sees and annotates directly into their view — one expert supporting a distributed field force.

Digital twins

Live, spatially accurate replicas of lines, facilities and equipment, driven by real sensor data.

Mobile & WebAR

No-install experiences that run from a browser on the phone a customer already owns.

Headset applications

Native builds for current-generation mixed-reality hardware, designed for shift-length use.

04 — VR Training & Simulation

Rehearse the thing that cannot go wrong.

Some procedures are too dangerous, too expensive or too rare to practise for real. Simulation removes that constraint — and, unlike a classroom, it produces evidence.

High-consequence procedure training

Emergency response, hazardous maintenance and clinical procedures rehearsed to fluency before anyone is at risk.

Scenario authoring

Tools that let your own trainers build and vary scenarios, so the programme does not stall waiting on a vendor.

Assessment analytics

Objective scoring on sequence, timing, hesitation and error recovery — competence you can report, not attendance you can only count.

Soft-skills simulation

Difficult conversations, de-escalation and safety briefings practised against AI-driven characters that respond to what the trainee actually says.

05 — Agentic & Conversational AI

Assistants that do the work, not just describe it.

A chatbot answers. An agent retrieves, reasons, calls your systems and completes the task — within boundaries you define and can audit.

Internal copilots

Assistants grounded in your policies, documentation and operational data, built for the teams who currently search three systems to answer one question.

Customer-facing agents

Support and sales agents that resolve rather than deflect, with a clean escalation path to a person the moment confidence drops.

Multilingual voice

Speech interfaces across Indian languages, for users who will never type their question.

Tool & system integration

Agents connected to your ERP, CRM and ticketing through governed, permissioned interfaces.

Guardrails & audit

Scope limits, refusal behaviour, logging and human review built in — not retrofitted after an incident.

06 — Data & Decision Intelligence

The foundation everything else stands on.

No model outperforms the data beneath it. We build the pipelines first — and then turn the behavioural signal that immersive systems generate into something leadership can act on.

Data engineering

Ingestion, cleaning, lineage and governance across the systems your operation already runs on.

Interaction analytics

Where attention went, where hesitation happened, where a procedure broke down — signal a dashboard was never designed to capture.

Decision dashboards

Reporting built around the decision being made, with the honest caveats attached to every number.

Immersive visualisation

Complex, multi-dimensional datasets explored in 3D space, where scale and relationship become obvious rather than inferred.
