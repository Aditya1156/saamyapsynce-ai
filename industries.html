Industries

The sectors where
precision pays.

We focus where the gap between a good decision and a poor one is measured in safety, downtime or capital — because that is where intelligence and immersion justify themselves fastest.

01 — Manufacturing & Industry 4.0

Quality and uptime
that do not depend on the shift.

A line generates far more signal than any team can watch. Vision models catch what tired eyes miss; spatial instructions put the same standard of guidance in front of every operator, every time.

Automated visual inspection

Defect detection at line speed, trained on your classes and calibrated to the tolerance your customers enforce.

Predictive maintenance

Sensor and acoustic models that flag degradation before it becomes an unplanned stop.

AR-guided assembly

Step-by-step overlays anchored to the workpiece, cutting changeover time and rework on complex variants.

Digital twins

Live replicas of cells and lines for simulation, bottleneck analysis and operator onboarding.

02 — Healthcare & Life Sciences

Build clinical skill
without clinical risk.

Competence comes from repetition that real patients cannot safely supply. Simulation provides it, and AI makes the feedback immediate and objective instead of dependent on a supervisor's availability.

Procedural rehearsal in VR

Surgical and emergency procedures practised to fluency, with scoring on sequence, timing and error recovery.

Imaging decision support

Vision models that prioritise and annotate studies, supporting the clinician's judgement rather than replacing it.

Patient education in 3D

Conditions and procedures explained spatially, improving understanding and informed consent.

Documentation assistants

Multilingual clinical note-taking that returns time to consultation rather than to the keyboard.

03 — Education & Skilling

The same quality of teaching,
wherever the learner is.

India's skilling challenge is one of scale and consistency. Immersive labs and adaptive tutoring deliver equivalent instruction to a learner in a tier-three town and a learner in a metro.

Immersive laboratories

Science and engineering practicals that need no physical equipment, no consumables and no safety officer.

Adaptive learning paths

Pacing and difficulty that respond to each learner's demonstrated grasp, not to a fixed syllabus clock.

Vocational simulation

Trades practised in VR — welding, wiring, machine operation — before a trainee touches live equipment.

Competency analytics

Institution-level visibility into what has genuinely been learned, broken down by skill rather than by attendance.

04 — Retail & Commerce

Let customers evaluate
before they commit.

Most returns are a failure of evaluation, not of product. Spatial preview and intelligent assistance close the gap between what a customer imagined and what arrives.

AR try-on & placement

Browser-based preview of furniture, eyewear, apparel and appliances in the customer's own space.

Demand & assortment planning

Forecasting at SKU and store level, accounting for promotion, season and local pattern.

Conversational shopping

Assistants that understand catalogue, stock and policy, and can complete the transaction.

Store analytics

Footfall, dwell and attention patterns captured with privacy boundaries agreed in advance.

05 — Logistics & Field Services

The expert is never
where the fault is.

Travel is the hidden cost of a distributed operation. Remote assistance and hands-free guidance let a single specialist support a field force spread across a state.

AR remote assistance

A specialist annotates directly into the technician's view, resolving first-time-fix without a site visit.

Route & fleet optimisation

Models that account for real road conditions, delivery windows and vehicle constraints.

Hands-free warehousing

Picking and put-away guided through the headset, keeping both hands on the work.

Condition assessment

Automated damage and quality grading from photographs at handover points.

06 — Energy & Infrastructure

Remote assets.
Hazardous inspections.

When assets are dispersed and inspection is dangerous, the value of seeing without travelling — and of rehearsing an emergency before it happens — is immediate.

Automated asset inspection

Drone and edge vision for towers, lines, pipelines and solar fields, with defect classification built in.

Safety & emergency drills

High-risk scenarios rehearsed in VR at a frequency live drills could never sustain.

Load & generation forecasting

Models combining weather, demand history and asset state for planning and trading decisions.

Immersive control rooms

Network state visualised spatially, so scale and cascade become visible rather than inferred.
